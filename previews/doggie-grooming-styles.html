Services

Grooming that keeps them happy and healthy.

Every package is customized to your pet's coat and needs — because good grooming isn't just about looking good, it's part of keeping them healthy.

Bath & Full Groom

Bath with shampoo and conditioner, blow dry, plus a customized trim and style — undercoat removal, knot removal, and clipping included.

Bath & Brush-Out

Bath, blow dry, nail clipping, ear cleaning, and a light brush — a lighter refresh between full grooms.

A La Carte

Nail trims (walk-ins welcome), teeth brushing, feather extensions, color highlights, and gland expression.
