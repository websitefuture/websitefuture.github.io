About the shop

Family-owned since 1972.

De Anza Appliance was founded in 1972 by William Boucher and has stayed family-owned and operated ever since. Between our technicians, the shop carries more than 100 combined years of hands-on appliance repair experience — the kind of institutional knowledge that comes from working on the same neighborhoods' washers, dryers, and refrigerators for decades.

Our approach hasn't changed much since the 1970s: pre-screen the problem before we book the visit, send a technician who calls ahead, diagnose it correctly, and charge a fair price for the fix. No guessing games, no parts-cannon repairs.

We're an authorized service provider for Liebherr refrigeration and General Electric appliances, and we work on most major household brands beyond that.
