Our Story

Baked by the same family for generations.

Greenlee's Bakery has been family owned and operated for 99 years, passed down from generation to generation right here in San Jose. What started as a neighborhood bakery on The Alameda has stayed exactly that — a place where the ovens are lit before dawn and the recipes are the ones the family has always trusted.

“Family owned and operated for 99 years, from generation to generation.”

Today the case still fills every morning with the same brioche-style cinnamon bread the bakery became known for, alongside loaf cakes, breakfast burritos, and pastries made by hand, plus a full breakfast and lunch menu for guests who want to sit down and stay a while.

On The Menu

What we bake, every single day.

A working bakery menu — breads and pastries from the case, plus a full breakfast and lunch line for guests who want to sit down.

Artisan Cinnamon Bread

The bakery's signature loaf — brioche-style cinnamon bread, plus blueberry cinnamon and chocolate varieties, baked fresh each morning.

Loaf Cakes & Bars

Burnt almond cake, lemon bars, and coffee cake, made in small batches alongside our daily bread bake.

Breakfast Burritos

Fresh cracked eggs, seasoned potatoes, and cheese with your choice of bacon, ham, chorizo, sausage, or steak.

Handmade Pastries

Bear claws, cheese danishes, cinnamon rolls, beignets, and croissants, laminated and shaped by hand.

Sandwiches & Bagels

Grilled steak and chicken sandwiches, BLTs, and grilled cheese, plus fresh bagels with cream cheese.

Cookies & Sweets

Chocolate chunk, salted caramel, and oatmeal cookies by the piece or the dozen.

Catering available for birthdays, holidays, corporate events, and weddings. Call or email to place an order.
