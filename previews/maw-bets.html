Our Story

Maw Bet's, for Betty.

We're named for our family matriarch, Betty Wideman Lawson — a daughter, sister, wife, mother, grandmother, and great-grandmother who called Nauvoo home for most of her life. Even with a big family of her own, she always opened her door and made room at her table for anyone who needed it.

That's the spirit behind the food here. Nothing's pre-made or shipped in — it's cooked fresh, to order, by people who grew up in this town. Someone from the family is usually right there on the floor, checking on tables and making sure the ranch is fresh.
