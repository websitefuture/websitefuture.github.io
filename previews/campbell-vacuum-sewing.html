About the Shop

A repair shop built around two crafts.

Campbell Vacuum & Sewing Services is a San Jose repair shop specializing in two things: vacuum cleaners and sewing machines. Most shops pick one — this one has built its reputation doing both well, working on everything from everyday household vacuums to home sewing machines that families rely on for years.

The shop is an authorized service dealer for Dyson and Electrolux, meaning warranty and manufacturer-spec repairs are handled in-house rather than shipped off and sent back weeks later.

  • Vacuum cleaner repair & service
  • Sewing machine repair & sales
  • Authorized Dyson & Electrolux service dealer

Why San Jose brings machines here

When a vacuum loses suction or a sewing machine starts skipping stitches, it's rarely worth tossing — it's usually a belt, a motor brush, a timing adjustment, or a part that a general handyman won't stock. This shop specializes in exactly those repairs.

Walk-ins are welcome during business hours, and the shop is easy to find right on Monterey Hwy.
