Our Story

Seventy years on South Second Street.

Tire Outlet Auto Service has been a fixture of San Jose's Santa Clara Valley since 1956 — a family business built on straight answers and steady hands. Michael Sanchez has owned and operated the shop since 1994, carrying the same standards forward for a new generation of customers.

The shop's focus has always been steering, suspension, and brake service — the systems that keep a vehicle safe and predictable, not just running. That specialty sits alongside a full slate of general repair and maintenance work, handled by the same crew you'll talk to at the counter.
