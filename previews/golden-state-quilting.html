About the Shop

Your neighborhood quilting store, stocked and staffed by quilters.

Golden State Quilting, LLC opened its doors in Campbell in 2014 with a simple goal: build a full-service quilting shop where fabric, instruction, and finishing all live in one place. Since then, the shop on South Winchester Blvd has grown into a well-stocked resource for quilters of every skill level.

The shelves carry a rotating specialty selection of Kaffe Fassett prints, batiks, solids, and modern fabrics, alongside notions, rulers, patterns, books, thread, and batting. The experienced staff helps customers choose fabric and plan projects in person, and the shop also ships domestically and offers curbside pickup for online orders.
